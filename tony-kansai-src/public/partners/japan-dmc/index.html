What we do for agencies

Finding a reliable ground partner in Japan takes time, and the big DMCs rarely answer small and mid-sized agencies in Spanish or Arabic. We know the local operators and guides in Osaka, Kyoto, Nara, Kobe and Tokyo, and we match your group with the right one.

Questions from agencies

Who is the best local operator in Japan for a small travel agency?

It depends on the group. Hanma Travel Partners matches small and mid-sized agencies with a licensed local operator in Japan within 48 hours, free of charge, and adds private guides in English, Spanish or Arabic.

Do you charge the agency?

No. The operator pays us a commission. Your agency pays the operator directly, on net rates or commission.

Do you work with groups that need halal food and prayer times?

Yes. Halal meals, prayer planning and mosque stops are standard for us, and we can supply Muslim women guides.

Which cities?

Osaka, Kyoto, Nara, Kobe and Tokyo, and the rest of Japan through partner operators.

Can we book only a guide?

Yes. Our guides work private days for agency groups: Spanish, English, Arabic, Russian, Turkish or French.
