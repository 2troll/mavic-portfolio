Why agencies ask for this

Muslimah groups and families often ask their agency for a woman guide, and in Japan they are hard to find. Japan ranks 7th among non-OIC destinations in the Global Muslim Travel Index 2026, but its weakest pillar is communication (31 out of 100). A Muslim guide who speaks your clients' language closes that gap.

Questions from agencies

Do we contact the guides directly?

No. Every request goes to Tony first on WhatsApp or email. He assigns the guide and stays responsible for the booking.

Do you give net rates?

Yes. We send a net rate per group and per day so your agency can add its own margin.

Which cities do you cover?

Osaka, Kyoto, Nara and Kobe with women guides; Tokyo with a Muslim male guide.

How far ahead should we book?

The guides are freelance: 2 to 4 weeks ahead for groups, longer for spring and autumn.

What is not included?

The rate covers guiding. Transport, entrance tickets and meals are paid separately; the guide can arrange them.
