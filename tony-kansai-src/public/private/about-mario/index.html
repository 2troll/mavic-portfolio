Meet Mario

Mario is a professional private tour guide who has guided English-speaking travelers in Japan for four years. He also speaks Spanish, is based in Kansai and married into a Japanese family. Day tours in Kyoto, Osaka and Nara are his home ground; beyond Kansai he travels with you, and multi-day journeys across Japan are his favorite way to guide.

Being part of a Japanese family has given Mario an understanding of daily life that goes beyond sightseeing: how a household marks the seasons, what is polite at a shrine or a dinner table, and how to ask for something in a way that is gladly received. He shares that with guests naturally, as the day goes on.

His great strength is organization. Mario plans the whole day, or the whole trip; he times trains and transfers, reserves restaurants and experiences in Japanese, and adapts on the spot when the weather turns or a sight is busier than expected.
