Mario’s favorite

Multi-day journeys with Mario

Mario is based in Kansai, but what he loves most is traveling with you across Japan: one guide, one continuous story, and every train, transfer and table arranged from the first morning to the last.

A sample ten-day journey

  1. Days 1–3 · TokyoSenso-ji at opening, the Imperial Palace gardens, Meiji Jingu, a sushi counter booked in Japanese; IC cards and trains explained on day one.
  2. Day 4 · Hakone or Mount FujiSuitcases forwarded to Kyoto; Lake Ashi or the Chureito pagoda, then a ryokan night with kaiseki dinner.
  3. Days 5–7 · Kyoto and NaraFushimi Inari at first light, Kiyomizu-dera, a private tea ceremony, Arashiyama, and a morning with Nara’s deer and the Great Buddha.
  4. Day 8 · Hiroshima and MiyajimaThe Peace Memorial Park and Museum, then Itsukushima Shrine, timed around the tide.
  5. Days 9–10 · Osaka or HimejiOsaka Castle and Kuromon Market, or Himeji Castle and Kobe, before departure from Kansai International Airport.

Meet Mario

Your guide, and the person who makes it all run

For four years, Mario has guided English-speaking travelers privately through Japan. He also speaks Spanish, so mixed-language families are at ease.

He is based in Kansai and married into a Japanese family. That gives him something no guidebook can: an everyday feel for how things are done, and the confidence to call a restaurant, a ryokan or a tea master in Japanese on your behalf.

Above all, Mario is an outstanding organizer. He times trains and transfers, books what needs booking, and quietly changes the plan when the weather or your mood suggests something better.

    Questions

    Frequently asked

    How much does a private guide with Mario cost?

    Rates are per private party, not per person, for day tours from his base in Kansai: half day (4 hours) ¥45,600, full day (8 hours) ¥69,600, long day trips such as Hiroshima and Miyajima, Mount Fuji, Nikko from Tokyo or Koyasan from ¥84,000, and extra hours ¥7,200. Outside Kansai, his train fare (and hotel if overnight) is added at cost. Multi-day journeys are quoted on request.

    What is included in the price?

    Mario’s time for your private party, planning before the trip, reservations made in Japanese and coordination on the day. Trains, admissions, meals and any private car with driver are paid separately, at cost. Cancellation is free up to 72 hours before.

    Can Mario book restaurants and a tea ceremony?

    Yes. He reserves kaiseki restaurants, sushi counters, tea ceremonies and other experiences in Japanese, explains dietary needs to the kitchen in advance, and times each booking around the day.

    Can we have a private car with driver?

    Yes. Mario arranges a car with driver on request, paid separately at cost. It is ideal for full days in Kyoto, Mount Fuji from Tokyo, families and guests who prefer less walking.

    Can Mario travel with us for several days?

    Yes, it is his favorite way to guide. Mario is based in Kansai and travels with you between cities anywhere in Japan. You pay the daily rate for each guided day, plus his train fares and hotel nights at cost; each journey is quoted on request.

    Is Mario a good guide for families with children?

    Yes. Days are paced in shorter blocks with breaks and meals planned in advance, and because rates are per private party, a guide is especially good value for families.

    We prefer a slower pace. Is that possible?

    Of course. Every day is private and planned around you: fewer sights, longer lunches, gardens instead of crowds. Mario adapts on the spot if you want to slow down further.
