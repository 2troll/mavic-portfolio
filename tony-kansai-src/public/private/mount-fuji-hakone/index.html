Mount Fuji & Hakone

Highlights with Mario

  • Lake Kawaguchi

    The easiest of the Fuji Five Lakes to reach, with the mountain reflected on still mornings.

  • Oshino Hakkai

    Eight clear ponds fed by Fuji’s snowmelt, filtered through lava for decades. Best visited early, before the coach groups.

  • Lake Ashi

    A caldera lake with Fuji to the west, crossed by sightseeing boats between Hakone-machi and Togendai.

  • Owakudani

    A steaming volcanic valley reached by ropeway, where eggs boiled in the hot springs turn black. Access depends on volcanic activity.

  • Hakone Shrine

    A cedar-shaded shrine whose Torii of Peace stands in the water of Lake Ashi.

A suggested day

How the day could unfold

A starting point, not a script: Mario adjusts the order and pace to the season, the crowds and your interests.

  1. 07:30Hotel lobby pick-up in Tokyo by car with driver, or a reserved limited express from Shinjuku.
  2. 09:30The 398 steps to the Chureito pagoda for the classic view of Fuji, while the sky is clearest.
  3. 11:00Oshino Hakkai’s spring-fed ponds.
  4. 12:30Lunch by Lake Kawaguchi; hoto noodles are the local specialty.
  5. 14:00The lakeshore and a viewpoint chosen for the day’s light and cloud.
  6. 16:00Return to Tokyo, arriving in the early evening, traffic depending.

Insider tips

What Mario wants you to know

  • Fuji hides in cloud more often than not, especially in summer. Mario watches the forecast and, if needed, swaps the day with another on your itinerary.
  • Combining Hakone and the Fuji Five Lakes in one day is possible by car but rushed; choose one, or stay a night.
  • A night in a Hakone ryokan with a private onsen and kaiseki dinner is one of the great experiences of Japan; the best rooms book months ahead.
  • Luggage forwarding lets you travel to Hakone with a small bag while suitcases go straight to your Kyoto hotel.

Hakone or Kawaguchiko: which is better?

Kawaguchiko has the closest, most iconic views of Fuji; Hakone has more variety, with the lake, ropeway, art museums and ryokan. For one day focused on the mountain, choose Kawaguchiko.

What if Mount Fuji is covered by clouds?

It happens often. Mario checks the forecast and webcams, and if the outlook is poor he suggests switching days or adjusting the route toward sights that are beautiful in any weather.
