Why a private guide works for US families

Kyoto is busy, signs are mostly in Japanese, and train transfers eat time. With a private guide you skip the guesswork: Tony meets you at the hotel, handles the trains and IC cards with you, picks the order that avoids the worst crowds, and changes the plan when the kids need a break.

Questions from US travelers

How much does a private guide in Kyoto cost?

One price for your whole group of up to 6 people: half day ¥38,000, full day ¥58,000, long day trips from ¥70,000 (base rate). In cherry blossom season (15 March - 25 April) it is ¥49,000 / ¥75,000 / from ¥91,000.

Can I pay from the US?

Yes. Pay in cash in yen on the day, or in advance with Wise, Revolut, PayPay or bank transfer once the date is confirmed on WhatsApp.

Do I need to tip?

No. Tipping is not customary in Japan, and the price is the full guide fee.

What is the time difference for planning calls?

Japan is 14 hours ahead of New York in winter (13 in summer) and 17 hours ahead of Los Angeles (16 in summer). WhatsApp messages are answered in Japan time.

When should I book for cherry blossom season?

As early as you can. Late March and early April is the busiest time of the year in Kyoto and the first dates fill up months ahead.

Is it OK with small kids or grandparents?

Yes. It is a private tour, so the pace, breaks and walking distance are set for your group.

What is the cancellation policy?

Free up to 72 hours before. With less than 72 hours notice, half the price is charged.
