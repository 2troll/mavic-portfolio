Included

  • A day planned for you: route, timings and stops worked out before we set off.
  • Pick-up in your hotel lobby.
  • I show you how to get around: IC card, trains and buses.
  • Where to eat well, including halal or vegetarian if you need it.

Not included: paid separately

  • I don't sell train tickets, hotel rooms or admission tickets.

What you can do in each city

Starting points from Osaka; we adjust the day to you. The price is the guide fee, per group.

  • Kyoto

    ≈ 30 min by train

    The old capital: temples, shrines and wooden streets. Best started early, before the tour groups arrive.

    • Half dayFushimi Inari and its thousands of torii, plus Kiyomizu-dera.
      ¥38,000
    • Full dayAll of that, plus the lanes of Gion and the Golden Pavilion (Kinkaku-ji).
      ¥58,000

  • Osaka

    Your hotel is here

    The city of food and of the castle. We get around on foot and by metro, at the pace locals do.

    • Half dayOsaka Castle and its park.
      ¥38,000
    • Full dayThe castle, Kuromon Market, Dōtonbori and Shinsekai.
      ¥58,000

  • Nara

    ≈ 40 min by train

    Japan's first capital. Deer roam the park freely, and the Great Buddha sits in one of the largest wooden buildings in the world.

    • Half dayNara Park, the deer and Tōdai-ji.
      ¥38,000
    • Full dayAll of that, plus Kasuga Taisha shrine and Isui-en garden.
      ¥58,000

  • Himeji

    ≈ 1 h by rapid train

    Japan's best-preserved original castle, a UNESCO World Heritage Site since 1993.

    • Full dayThe castle and Kōko-en garden, with a stop in Kobe on the way back if you like.
      ¥58,000

  • Kobe

    ≈ 25 min by train

    A port city between the sea and the mountains, and home to the Kobe Mosque, built in 1935, the oldest in Japan.

    • Half dayMeriken harbour, the Kitano quarter and the mosque.
      ¥38,000
    • Full dayAll of that, plus Nankinmachi Chinatown and the Shin-Kobe ropeway.
      ¥58,000

Questions

Is the price per person?

No, per group: the same for 1 as for 6 people. Half day ¥38,000, full day ¥58,000 and 2 hours in Kyoto ¥25,000.

Does the guide fee include tickets, trains or meals?

No. You pay the guide only for the guiding. Tickets, trains, buses, meals and experiences are paid separately, on the spot or to the partner.

How much is a hiking guide near Kyoto or Osaka?

¥68,000 per group for a guided hiking day (Atago, Hiei, Rokko, Kongo and more). Transport and food extra.

How much do kimono or the tea ceremony cost?

Whatever each partner charges on their website: paid separately, directly to them. Some give you a discount when you come with me.

How do I pay?

Payment is in cash, in yen, on the day, or in advance with PayPal, Wise or bank transfer. If you pay in advance, do it only once we have confirmed your date on WhatsApp.
