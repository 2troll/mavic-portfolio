Questions

What does the price include?

What each trip lists. All are private unless stated; in Türkiye the packages include lunches and dinners, and the 7-day trip includes domestic flights. International flights are not included. Before you book, Tony sends you the itinerary and the full list of inclusions.

How do I book?

Message Tony on WhatsApp (+34 634 193 106) with the trip, your dates and group size. He confirms availability and a fixed price, and explains how to pay.

Is there halal food?

Yes. In Bosnia and Herzegovina and in Türkiye halal food is the norm; in Sri Lanka halal meals are available. For the other routes, tell Tony what you need and he checks before you book.

Who runs the trip?

A licensed local operator in each country. Tony coordinates it with you in your language (English, Spanish or Arabic) and is your contact for the whole trip.

Prices checked on 2026-11-13. They may change with dates and season; Tony confirms one fixed price before you book.

Interested in one of them?

Tell me the trip, your dates and group size, and I’ll reply with a fixed price.
