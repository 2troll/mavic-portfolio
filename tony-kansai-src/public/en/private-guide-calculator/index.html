    Questions

    How much is a private guide in Kyoto?

    ¥38,000 for a half day (4 h) and ¥58,000 for a full day (8 h), per group of up to 6 (base rate). Cherry blossom +30 %, autumn +20 %, winter +10 %. Tickets, transport and meals extra.

    When is a private guide worth it?

    When you have 1-2 days in Kyoto, travel with children or older relatives, it is your first time in Japan, or you want Kyoto and Nara in one day.

    And when is it not?

    With 3+ days, no rush and no special needs, you can go on your own with a good guidebook.

    Is there anything shorter than a half day?

    Yes: the 2-hour tour is ¥25,000 per group of up to 6. Good for Gion at sunset, Fushimi Inari early or Kiyomizu.

    Where does the tour start?

    I meet you in your hotel lobby in Kyoto or Osaka, and the day is for your group only.

    How do I pay?

    Cash on the day, or in advance with Wise, Revolut or PayPay.

    What if I need to cancel?

    Free cancellation up to 72 hours before; if your flight is delayed, we find another day.

    What if we are more than 6?

    Up to 14 people on request (with a second guide or a supplement).

    And long day trips?

    Kōyasan, Amanohashidate or Kumano, from ¥70,000 per group.
