Iheb’s card-buying rules

  • Buy in a real shop that gives a receipt: the best protection against fakes.
  • Inspect every expensive card before paying.
  • Keep your receipts for the tax refund when you leave Japan.
  • Buy to collect and enjoy, not as an investment.

FAQ

Is there an anime and Pokémon tour guide in Tokyo?

Yes. Iheb, who lives in Japan, is a professional in anime, Japanese pop culture and Pokémon card collecting, and runs private tours in English (also French and Arabic) in Tokyo and around. Book on WhatsApp: +81 80 8015 5322.

Where can I buy Pokémon cards in Tokyo?

Sealed products (booster packs, boxes, accessories) at the official Pokémon Centers; single cards, new or used, at specialist shops in Akihabara, Nakano and Ikebukuro. Buy in a real shop that gives you a receipt and check every expensive card before paying.

Can I use Japanese Pokémon cards in tournaments back home?

Usually not: Japanese-language cards are generally not allowed in official tournaments outside Japan. For collecting, they are hugely popular. Check the current rules before you buy for play.

How do I get into the Pokémon Café?

Reservation only, on the official website. Slots open at 6 pm Japan time and sell out very fast.

How do I get Ghibli Museum tickets?

They are not sold at the museum. Tickets for each month go on sale on the 10th of the previous month at 10 am Japan time on Lawson Ticket.

How much does the tour cost?

It depends on group size, length and places. Iheb sends you a price on WhatsApp once he knows your plan.

Is it family friendly?

Yes. It is a private tour just for your family, at your pace. Halal-friendly meals and prayer breaks can be added for Muslim travelers.
