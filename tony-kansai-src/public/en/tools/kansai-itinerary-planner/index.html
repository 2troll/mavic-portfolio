How the plan is ordered

  1. Kyoto comes first, a full day with a guide: it is the hardest to organise and it sets you up to get around on your own for the rest of the trip.
  2. Nara, half a day: the park, the deer and Tōdai-ji fit into a morning; the afternoon is free.
  3. Hiroshima and Miyajima always go on the same day, never split: the city in the morning and the island in the afternoon, timed to the tide.
  4. Long day trips (Hiroshima and Miyajima, Kōyasan) and hiking only come in with 4 days or more, so the trip is not spent on trains. With fewer than 6 days, one at most.
  5. If the last day would be a long trip, a day near your hotel moves to the end, so you are not rushing.
  6. A guide only on the days where it really helps; Osaka, Arashiyama and Uji are easy on your own. If you tick food or children, Osaka gets a guide; if you tick halal, Kobe and its mosque.

Questions

How many days do I need in Kansai?

With 1 day, Kyoto. With 2, Kyoto and Nara. With 3 you see the essentials: Kyoto, Nara and Osaka. With 5 you add Himeji and Hiroshima and Miyajima, and with 7 also Arashiyama and Kobe, without rushing.

Is it better to stay in Osaka or Kyoto?

Osaka has direct trains to almost everything: Kyoto in ≈ 29 min for ¥580, Nara, Kobe, Himeji and the airport. Kyoto puts you inside the temples first thing in the morning. If you plan day trips, Osaka; if the trip is mostly Kyoto, Kyoto.

Which days are worth doing with a guide?

The first one, in Kyoto; Nara; and the long days such as Hiroshima and Miyajima or Kōyasan. Osaka, Arashiyama and Uji are easy on your own; with children or if you are into food, a guided Osaka day pays off too.

How much is the guide for several days?

Each day is charged per group, not per person: half day ¥38,000, full day ¥58,000, long day trip from ¥70,000. For several days I send you one fixed price for the whole trip.

Can Hiroshima and Miyajima be done on different days?

Better not: they go together in one day, the city in the morning and the island in the afternoon. From Shin-Osaka it is ≈ 1 h 25 min on the Nozomi shinkansen (¥10,950 with a reserved seat).

What if we travel with children?

Nara is the favourite day with children because of the deer, and Osaka works very well too. The pace adapts to them and we skip Kyoto’s crowded buses.

Is there halal food and are there mosques?

Yes. There are halal-certified restaurants near Gion in Kyoto and in Namba in Osaka, and Kobe has Japan’s oldest mosque, built in 1935. If you tick “Halal and mosques”, the plan includes Kobe with a guide.

Do you pick us up at the airport?

No: I am a day guide and I do not do airport transfers. I meet you in your hotel lobby in Osaka or Kyoto.
