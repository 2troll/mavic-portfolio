Osaka to Kyoto by train: 29 min and ¥580 on JR, or ¥410 on Hankyu

Choose where you leave from and where you are going, and you get every train that works: line, departure and arrival station, minutes, adult fare, whether ICOCA works and whether the JR Pass covers it. There are 44 routes between Osaka, Kyoto, Nara, Kobe, Himeji, Hiroshima, Uji, Fushimi Inari, Arashiyama, Kōyasan and Kansai Airport, each checked on 16 Nov 2026. The cheapest from Osaka to Kyoto is Hankyu (¥410); the fastest is the shinkansen from Shin-Osaka (13 min).

ICOCA: the card that works almost everywhere

ICOCA is JR West’s transport card. You top it up at station machines and tap through the gates without buying a ticket on JR, Hankyu, Hanshin, Keihan, Kintetsu, Nankai and the Osaka Metro. Tokyo’s Suica works here too.

What it does not do: pay for the shinkansen with stored value (you need a ticket, or a smartEX booking with your registered card). On the Haruka, the rapi:t and Kintetsu limited expresses, the card pays the fare but the limited express ticket is bought separately.

On the Randen tram to Arashiyama, the Kōyasan cable car and the airport bus I could not confirm it in an official source: ask at the station before boarding.

When the JR Pass is not worth it in Kansai

The nationwide JR Pass only works on JR trains. Hankyu, Hanshin, Keihan, Kintetsu and Nankai are other companies, and they are often the best trains in Kansai: Hankyu is the cheapest to Kyoto and Kintetsu drops you next to Nara Park. And trips here are short and cheap: Osaka–Kyoto ¥580, Osaka–Nara ¥840, Kyoto–Nara ¥720, Osaka–Himeji ¥1,460.

If you only travel within Kansai, add up your trains with the table and compare with the current pass price on its official site (japanrailpass.net): the pass usually comes out more expensive. It starts to pay off when the same pass covers long shinkansen trips such as Tokyo or Hiroshima; and remember the Nozomi needs a separate special ticket.

For Kansai there is JR West’s WEST QR Kansai Area Pass. According to its official site it costs ¥2,700 for one day, ¥4,700 for two, ¥5,500 for three and ¥6,700 for four (adult); it is only sold by travel agencies outside Japan and only to temporary visitors. It covers JR rapid and local trains in its area and the Haruka in non-reserved seats; not the shinkansen.

Rush hour

If you can, avoid weekday trains roughly from 7:30 to 9:00: they are packed and luggage makes it miserable. They also fill up in the early evening when people head home. If you want a guaranteed seat, look at Keihan’s Premium Car, JR’s A-Seat or a reserved seat on the shinkansen: they all cost extra.

Which train suits where you stay

Umeda (Osaka Station)
JR to Kyoto, Kobe, Himeji and Nara; Hankyu to central Kyoto, Arashiyama and Kobe; Hanshin to Kobe. Keihan leaves from Yodoyabashi, 2 minutes away by subway.
Namba
Kintetsu direct to Nara, Nankai to the airport and Kōyasan, and Hanshin to Kobe. For Kyoto, the Midōsuji subway to Umeda or Yodoyabashi.
Shin-Osaka
The shinkansen to Kyoto, Himeji and Hiroshima, and the Haruka to the airport.
Kyoto Station
JR to Nara, Fushimi Inari, Uji and Arashiyama, and the Haruka to the airport. The Kintetsu to Nara leaves from the same station.
Gion or Kawaramachi (Kyoto)
Keihan to Fushimi Inari, Uji and Osaka; Hankyu to Osaka and Arashiyama.

Questions

How much is the train from Osaka to Kyoto?

Per adult: Hankyu from Osaka-umeda to Kyoto-kawaramachi ¥410 (≈ 43 min); Keihan from Yodoyabashi to Gion-shijō ¥490 (≈ 50 min); JR from Osaka to Kyoto ¥580 (≈ 29 min). The shinkansen from Shin-Osaka costs ¥1,450 in a non-reserved seat and takes ≈ 13 min.

How do I get from Kansai Airport to Kyoto?

Without changing trains, on the Haruka: ¥3,060, ≈ 1 h 18 min. With one change at Osaka and no supplement, ¥1,860 and ≈ 1 h 57 min. On the Airport Limousine Bus, ¥2,800 and ≈ 1 h 28 min, depending on traffic.

And from the airport to Namba?

On the Nankai Airport Express, ¥970 and ≈ 45 min. On the rapi:t, ¥1,670 including the limited express ticket, with a reserved seat.

What is the fastest way to Nara?

From Namba, Kintetsu: ≈ 37 min and ¥680, and it drops you by the park. From Osaka Station, the JR Yamatoji Rapid: ≈ 50 min and ¥840. From Kyoto, the Kintetsu limited express: ≈ 33 min and ¥1,280.

Can I use ICOCA or Suica on every train?

On JR, Hankyu, Hanshin, Keihan, Kintetsu, Nankai and the Osaka Metro, yes. Not with stored value on the shinkansen: you need a ticket, or a smartEX booking with your registered card. On the Haruka and the rapi:t, the limited express ticket is separate.

Is the JR Pass worth it for Kansai?

Rarely, if you only travel within Kansai: trains are cheap and the best ones are often not JR. Add up your trips with the table and compare with the pass price on japanrailpass.net.

How do I get to Kōyasan?

From Namba, the Nankai Kōya Line to Gokurakubashi and the cable car up to Kōyasan: ¥1,430 and ≈ 1 h 51 min. On the Kōya limited express, ¥2,530. From the station a bus goes up to the town.

Do you pick us up at the airport?

No: I am a day guide and I do not do airport transfers. I meet you in your hotel lobby in Osaka or Kyoto on the day of the tour.
