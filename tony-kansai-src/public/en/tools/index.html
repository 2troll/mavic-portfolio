Frequently asked questions

Are the tools free?

Yes. They are all free and ask for no sign-up, email or payment. The Japanese cards work in your phone’s browser.

What do I do in a restaurant if I don’t speak Japanese?

Use the food card: tick whether you eat halal, no pork, no alcohol, vegetarian, vegan or which allergies you have, and show the waiter the full-screen card. If you’d rather point, the Yubisashi app has sheets for ordering with your finger.

How do I tell a taxi driver where I’m going?

With the taxi card: pick the destination (sights in Kyoto, Osaka, Nara, Kobe and Himeji, the airport or a mosque) or type your hotel, and the driver sees it in large Japanese.

Is what I type saved?

Only on your own phone, in the browser’s storage, so you don’t have to tick it again. It is not sent anywhere.

Is the Japanese correct?

They are short sentences in polite Japanese (the です/ます register a customer uses with a waiter or a taxi driver), with romaji and a translation on the same page.

How much does a private guide in Kansai cost?

Per group of up to 6: 2 hours in Kyoto ¥25,000, half day (4 h) ¥38,000, full day (8 h) ¥58,000 and long day trips from ¥70,000. Tickets, trains and meals extra.

Are there tools for Muslim travellers?

Yes: the food card has a halal option, the taxi card includes the Osaka Ibaraki and Kobe mosques, and there are Arabic pages on mosques and halal food in Kyoto and Osaka.
