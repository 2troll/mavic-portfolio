Good to know

Can I book a female Muslim guide in Japan?

Yes. We work with four Muslim women guides who live in Osaka and Kyoto. Send your dates and we confirm who is free.

Which languages do they speak?

English, Arabic, Indonesian, French and Japanese.

How do you handle prayer times?

The route is planned around the five prayers, with stops at mosques such as Kobe Mosque or at prayer rooms.

Is the food halal?

We choose halal-certified or Muslim-friendly restaurants in advance, and the guide checks ingredients such as mirin.

Do we take part in anything at temples?

No. At temples and shrines the guide only explains history and culture.

How much is it and when should I book?

We send a price for your group before you book. Book 1 to 2 weeks ahead if you can.
