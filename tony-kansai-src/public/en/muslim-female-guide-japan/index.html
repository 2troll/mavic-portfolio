Why a Muslim woman guide

Questions

Can I book a female Muslim guide in Japan?

Yes. We work with four Muslim women guides who live in Osaka and Kyoto. Tell us your dates and we confirm which one is free.

Which languages do they speak?

Arabic, Indonesian, English, French and Japanese. Malaysian guests are served in Indonesian, and Tony adds Spanish.

Can the group be women only?

Yes. Women-only tours with a woman guide are what this service is for. Families and couples are welcome too.

How do you handle prayer times?

The route is planned around the five daily prayers, with stops at mosques such as Osaka Ibaraki and Kobe Mosque or at prayer rooms.

Is the food halal?

We book halal-certified or Muslim-friendly restaurants in advance, and the guide checks ingredients such as mirin or sake in sauces.

Do we have to take part in anything at temples?

No. At temples and shrines the guide explains history and culture only. Nobody is asked to join any ritual.

How much does it cost and how early should I book?

We send a quote for your group before you book. The guides are freelance, so 1 to 2 weeks ahead is best.
