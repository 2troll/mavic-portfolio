We are looking for Muslim women guides in Japan

Muslim women and families visiting Osaka, Kyoto and Nara often ask us for a female guide who understands prayer times, halal food and modesty. If you live in Japan, guide with us.

The work

  • Private day tours for Muslim women, families and small groups in Osaka, Kyoto, Nara and Kobe.
  • Freelance: you choose the days you can work. No minimum.
  • Paid per tour day. Tell us your usual rate; we agree it before each tour.
  • For every tour you get the route, timings, halal restaurants and prayer spots.

What our guests book: private tours with a Muslim female guide, planned around mosques and prayer rooms in Kansai and halal food in Kyoto and Osaka. Many families also add a day in Kobe or a Nara day trip.

Who we are looking for

  • A Muslim woman who lives in Japan. Kansai is ideal; Tokyo is also welcome.
  • You speak English, Arabic, Malay/Indonesian, Turkish, French or Spanish. Japanese is a plus.
  • You know your city, you enjoy people and you are on time.
  • Your residence status allows this kind of work in Japan.
  • A guide licence (通訳案内士) is welcome but not required: since 2018 anyone may guide for pay in Japan.

How to apply

  1. Send a message

    Your name, city, languages, the days you are usually free, any guiding experience and, if you like, your daily rate.

  2. Short call

    We meet by video or voice call, 15 minutes, to get to know each other.

  3. First tour

    We match you with the next tour that fits your days and send you the full plan.

We do not publish your details. They are only used to contact you about tours.
