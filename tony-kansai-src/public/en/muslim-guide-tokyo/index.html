Yes, there is a Muslim tour guide in Tokyo: Iheb, from Tunisia, lives in Japan and guides private groups in English, Arabic and French. He plans every day around halal meals and prayer times, from Tokyo Camii and halal ramen to Mt. Fuji by private car (through licensed partners) and the anime and Pokémon shops kids love. Price on WhatsApp: +81 80 8015 5322.

What a Muslim-friendly day looks like

  1. Morning: Tokyo Camii, then the city’s highlights at your pace.
  2. Lunch: halal ramen, wagyu or another halal restaurant, booked ahead.
  3. Afternoon: shopping, Skytree or the anime districts, with a prayer break.
  4. Evening: a halal dinner close to your hotel.

Good to know

  • Read labels: many snacks contain pork extract, lard or mirin. Iheb helps you check in the shop.
  • Airports: Narita and Haneda have prayer rooms.
  • Best seasons: autumn colors in November, clear Fuji views in winter, cherry blossoms in late March and April.
  • All of Japan: Tokyo with Iheb; Kyoto and Osaka with Tony, who also speaks Arabic.

FAQ

Is there a Muslim tour guide in Tokyo?

Yes. Iheb is a Muslim guide from Tunisia who lives in Japan and runs private tours in English, Arabic and French in Tokyo, around Mt. Fuji, Shizuoka and Nagano. WhatsApp: +81 80 8015 5322.

Is it easy to find halal food in Tokyo?

Yes, more and more: halal ramen, halal wagyu, Indian, Turkish and Malaysian restaurants, plus Muslim-friendly menus in some hotels. Iheb picks places that fit your group and books ahead.

Where can we pray in Tokyo?

Tokyo Camii is the best-known mosque, and there are prayer rooms at Narita and Haneda airports and in some malls and stations. Iheb builds prayer times into the day.

Is Tokyo good for Muslim families with children?

Yes. It is safe and clean, and the anime, Pokémon and Nintendo shops are a favorite with kids. A private tour lets you set the pace and the breaks.

How much does it cost?

It depends on group size, days and transport. Iheb sends the price directly on WhatsApp.
