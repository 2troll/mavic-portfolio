Yes, there is a French-speaking guide in Tokyo: Iheb. He lives in Japan, guides private tours in French in Tokyo and the Kanto region (Kamakura, Yokohama, Nikko, Hakone), around Mount Fuji, and in Nagano and Shizuoka prefectures, and also speaks Arabic and English. He quotes a price per group on WhatsApp (+81 80 8015 5322).

Key facts

  • Languages: French; also Arabic and English.

Day trips from Tokyo

  • Kamakura and Yokohama: about 1 hour by train.
  • Nikko: about 1 h 50 from Asakusa.
  • Hakone or the Fuji Five Lakes: about 1 h 20 to 2 hours from Shinjuku.
  • Nagano (snow monkeys) and Shizuoka (Mount Fuji views): by Shinkansen.

Anime and Pokémon cards

Iheb is a professional in anime, Japanese pop culture and Pokémon card collecting. He builds the day around Akihabara, Nakano Broadway, Ikebukuro, the Pokémon Centers, the Ghibli Museum and real-life anime locations, in French: anime guide in Tokyo (in French).

Also going to Kyoto?

Iheb guides only in Tokyo and eastern Japan. For Kyoto and Osaka, Tony, from the same team, guides in Spanish, English and Arabic (Tony Kansai Guide).
