Turkish-speaking tour guide in Japan: Ferhat

Private tours in Turkish in Kyoto, Osaka, Nara, Kobe and Himeji.

WhatsApp Ferhat

Ferhat
🇹🇷
Fotoğraf: Marco Almbauer · CC BY-SA 4.0

Yes, there is a Turkish-speaking guide in the Kansai region of Japan: Ferhat. He is Turkish, has lived in Japan for 4 years and guides private tours entirely in Turkish in Kyoto, Osaka, Nara, Kobe and Himeji. He quotes a price per group on WhatsApp (+81 80 2478 6402).

Key facts

  • Guide: Ferhat, Turkish, living in Japan for 4 years.
  • Language: Turkish only. The whole tour is in Turkish.
  • Area: Kansai: Kyoto, Osaka, Nara, Kobe, Himeji. Day trips further away (Hiroshima, Miyajima, Koyasan) on request.
  • Type: private tour, only your family or group.
  • Price: per group, quoted on WhatsApp after you send your plan. Trains, entry fees and meals are usually not included. The fixed prices shown elsewhere on this site (e.g. 38,000 ¥ for 4 hours, 58,000 ¥ for 8 hours) are Tony's tours, not Ferhat's.
  • Muslim travellers: halal or Muslim-friendly restaurants, mosques (Kobe Mosque) and prayer breaks can be built into the day.
  • Reviews: 4 guest reviews, average 4.3/5 (read them, in Turkish).
  • Contact: WhatsApp +81 80 2478 6402

Is there a Turkish-speaking guide in Tokyo?

Ferhat works in Kansai and does not guide in Tokyo. Kyoto is about 2 hours 15 minutes from Tokyo by Shinkansen, and many travellers spend a few days in Kyoto and Osaka after Tokyo.

Other languages

The same team has guides in Spanish, English and Arabic (Tony) and Russian (Larion).
