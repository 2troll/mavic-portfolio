Autumn · November · early December

01

Eikan-dō at night

The shot
The pond reflecting the lit-up maples: the mirror shot.
When to go
Autumn night illumination; go right at opening.
Guide’s tip
Crouch and film the reflection with your phone almost touching the water.
02

Kiyomizu-dera

The shot
The wooden stage floating over a sea of maples.
When to go
First thing in the morning or at the autumn night illumination.
Guide’s tip
The best shot is from the viewpoint opposite, not from the stage itself.
03

Arashiyama bamboo grove

The shot
The bamboo tunnel, and the river with red hills.
When to go
Before 8:00; after that it is a corridor of people.
Guide’s tip
Pair it with Togetsukyō Bridge: maples behind, river in front.
