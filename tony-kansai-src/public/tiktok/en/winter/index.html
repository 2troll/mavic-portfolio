Lights, snow and matcha: TikTok’s winter

Winter is Kansai’s secret season: fewer people, night illuminations and, with luck, snowy Kyoto. Snowy temple videos are among the most searched.

December · January · February

01

Snowy Kyoto

The shot
The Golden Pavilion or Kiyomizu under snow: winter’s most wanted shot.
When to go
The morning after snowfall; it does not snow every year.
Guide’s tip
I watch the forecast with you and, if it snows, we switch the route that morning.
02

Fushimi Inari at dusk

The shot
The torii lit by lanterns, almost empty.
When to go
Late afternoon: in winter it gets dark early.
Guide’s tip
Wrap up warm: it is much colder higher up.
03

Osaka lit up

The shot
Winter illuminations and Osaka Castle at night.
When to go
December evenings; I’ll confirm each year’s dates.
Guide’s tip
Finish in Dōtonbori with fresh takoyaki: the perfect food video.
04

Arashiyama without crowds

The shot
The bamboo in silence, without the autumn crowds.
When to go
In the morning; winter light is soft all day.
Guide’s tip
The best time of year to film the bamboo empty: make the most of it.
05

Matcha in Uji

The shot
Hot matcha in Uji, the home of Japanese tea.
When to go
Any winter day, ideally a weekday.
Guide’s tip
Matcha is scarce and trending: I take you to real tea houses.
06

Nara in winter

The shot
The deer in their winter coats in front of Tōdai-ji.
When to go
In the morning, when the park is quiet.
Guide’s tip
Buy deer crackers and keep them hidden: if the deer see them, they surround you.
